> Connect your Gmail API

Follow these steps to generate your own API credentials. This ensures your data stays private and your rate limits are dedicated only to your account.

1

Create a New Project

Go to the Google Cloud Console and click on the project dropdown at the top. Select "New Project" and name it 2Fast-Auth.

2

Enable Gmail API

Navigate to "APIs & Services" > "Library". Search for "Gmail API" and click "Enable". This allows 2Fast to communicate with Google's servers.

3

Configure OAuth Consent Screen

Go to "OAuth consent screen". Choose "External" (unless you are a Workspace admin). Fill in required fields: App Name, User Support Email, and Developer Contact Info.

4

Add Scopes

Under "Scopes", add gmail.readonly. This grants read-only access to your OTP emails without allowing the app to send or delete mail.

5

Add Test Users

While in "Testing" mode, you must explicitly add your Gmail address to the "Test users" list. Only these users will be able to log in.

6

Create Credentials

Navigate to "Credentials" > "Create Credentials" > "OAuth client ID". Select "Desktop App" as the application type.

7

Name the Client

Name your client 2Fast Client. Click "Create".

8

Copy Client ID

A modal will appear with your Client ID. Copy it and paste it into the field at the bottom of this screen.

9

Copy Client Secret

Copy the "Client Secret" value. Treat this like a password—never share it with anyone.

10

Finalize & Connect

Enter the details below and click "Authorize Account". A browser window will open for final confirmation.
